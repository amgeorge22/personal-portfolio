FSAE Motor Mounting

Formula SAEFEATopology optimization6061 aluminum

As a part of Olin Electric Motorsports (Formula SAE), I designed and fabricated a mounting system that withstands load cases of up to 10,500 Newtons in order to safely secure our race car motor to the chassis. I conducted topology studies to optimize light-weighting of the mounting plates and utilized Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to ensure a minimum 1.5 factor of safety on our plates and brackets.
