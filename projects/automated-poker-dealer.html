Automated Poker Dealer

Mechanical designWiringArduinoTeam project

As a part of the class Principles of Integrated Engineering, my team and I went through the ideation, design, manufacturing, integration, and testing, of an automated poker dealing table. I specialized in our card dealing mechanism, which used a roller to shoot out one card at a time. I worked on the mechanical design, wiring, and coding/tuning of this subsystem.
