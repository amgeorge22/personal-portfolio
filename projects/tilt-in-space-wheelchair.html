Tilt-In-Space Wheelchair

OnshapeDesign for manufacturePrototypingKyaro Assistive Tech

For the class Mechanical Design, I worked in a team of four alongside Kyaro Assistive Tech (https://www.kyaroassistive.org) to create a “Tilt-In-Space” wheelchair. Our wheelchair is able to rotate backwards in increments of 15°, and lock in place at each increment. It is designed to be easy to manufacture (able to be machined within the shop capabilities in Tanzania) and easy to operate. To make it easy to manufacture, we modified the original Kyaro wheelchair design, ultimately creating an additional 5 unique parts to be added to the original design.

The wheelchair was designed in OnShape, and any unique parts and modifications to the original wheelchair were physically prototyped.

To learn more about the project, check out the website here: https://sites.google.com/view/tiltinspacewheelchair/home
