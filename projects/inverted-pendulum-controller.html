Inverted Pendulum Controller

MATLABSimulinkPID control

The final for our Engineering Systems Analysis class, affectionately referred to as Rocky, is creating an inverted pendulum controller. The team and I used MATLAB and Simulink to tune a PID controller to balance a robot that we modeled as an inverted pendulum system. To see the robot in action, our analysis of the system, and how we determined the values for the controller, see our report.
