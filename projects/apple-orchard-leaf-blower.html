Apple Orchard Leaf Blower

CAD3D printingCNC routingArduinoPython

As part of my work at Carnegie Mellon University's Field Robotics Institute, I contributed to an apple orchard scanning system that aimed to target locations for maintenance & fertilizer adjustment. I designed a leaf blowing mechanism in CAD to direct airflow and agitate a canopy to reveal hidden baby apples to a mounted camera. This allowed the system to achieve an accurate count of where and how many apples are in an orchard.

I manufactured the frame, air direction components, gears, & mounting supports using 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC routing & tested mechanism on artificial 1.5m x 1.5m canopy using Arduino & Python control
